# ♻️ Sortify – Smart Waste Sorting & Recycling App

Sortify is a smart recycling mobile application designed to guide users in sorting recyclable items correctly, encourage environmentally responsible behaviour, and create income opportunities through recycling and reuse.

The application supports the **3R concept (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle)** and aligns with the **United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)**, particularly:
- **SDG 12** – Responsible Consumption and Production  
- **SDG 8** – Decent Work and Economic Growth  

---

## 🌍 Problem Statement

Improper waste management remains a major challenge, especially in communities with limited access to recycling education and infrastructure.  
Many people:
- Do not know how to sort waste correctly
- Lack motivation to recycle
- See no financial value in proper waste management  

This results in recyclable materials being discarded improperly, environmental pollution, and missed income opportunities.

---

## 💡 Solution: Sortify

Sortify provides a **mobile-based, user-friendly platform** that combines:
- Waste sorting guidance
- Reward-based recycling
- A second-hand marketplace
- Community-driven sustainability

The app turns waste management into a **rewarding and engaging activity**.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🔍 Smart Waste Sorting Assistant
- Users search or select household items
- The app identifies whether the item is recyclable
- Displays the **correct recycling bin colour**
- Improves recycling accuracy and awareness

---

### 🏆 Recycling Rewards System
- Users upload images as proof of recycling
- Approved submissions earn **Sortify Points**
- Points are stored in an in-app wallet
- Points can be converted into cash after reaching milestones

---

### 🛒 Reusable Items Marketplace
- Buy and sell second-hand or reusable items
- Upload item images, descriptions, and prices
- Reduces waste sent to landfills
- Generates income for users and platform revenue through commissions

---

### 📊 Submission Status & History
- View recycling submission status (Pending / Approved / Rejected)
- Track points earned and submission history

---

## 👥 User Roles

### 👤 User
- Sort recyclable items
- Earn rewards
- Buy and sell reusable items
- Redeem points into cash

### 🛠️ Admin
- Review recycling submissions
- Approve or reject uploaded proofs
- Assign reward points

---

## 🧱 System Flow (Simplified)

1. User registers and logs in  
2. User sorts and submits recyclable items  
3. Admin reviews submissions  
4. Points are credited to user wallet  
5. Users redeem points or sell reusable items
